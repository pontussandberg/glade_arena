Sound recordings used by the game, all released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; it is given
here with thanks. Files were trimmed, mixed to mono and normalized; some were halved in
sample rate. The game layers and pitches them at play time (client/src/audio.rs).
